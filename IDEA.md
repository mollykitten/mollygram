Bring the beauty and convenience of the official macOS Swift Telegram client to Windows, Linux and macOS in one Flutter codebase, and then go further: a premium look, better animations, and a UX that is clearly ahead of the official client, taking the best from every custom client: exteraGram, Inugram, Swiftgram, AyuGram and the rest.

- Look and feel first: liquid glass on the floating layer, spring motion, interruptible transitions; every screen should feel better than TelegramSwift, not merely equal to it.
- The familiar Telegram Desktop layout, so nothing has to be relearned, on all three desktop platforms.
- Freedom to add our own features: tabs, split view, ⌘K palette, ghost mode, locally kept deleted messages and edit history, 120 fps video messages, uncompressed media.
- Every good feature from the forks is a candidate (docs/forks-research.md tracks them); a JavaScript plugin sandbox covers the long tail without forking again.
- Parity with the official client as the floor (docs/parity.md), never the ceiling.
